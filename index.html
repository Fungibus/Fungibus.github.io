Who we are

A small studio,
worker owned.

Chicken Coop Games is a worker owned cooperative. Everyone on the team has an equal say in what we make and how we run the studio.

We want to help connect people through the games we make.

Want to join the coop? We occasionally bring on new members. If you make games and believe in what we are building, we would love to hear from you.
